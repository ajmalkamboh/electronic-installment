# Architecture Decision Register (ADR & BDR)

This register tracks all critical architectural, structural, and business decisions for the **Electronic Installment SaaS** platform.

Statuses used:
- `CONFIRMED`: Formally decided and approved as baseline.
- `PROPOSED`: Architecturally recommended and awaiting formal sign-off.
- `REQUIRES BUSINESS DECISION`: Open question requiring client / stakeholder business decision before implementation.
- `FUTURE`: Deferred to a subsequent milestone or phase.
- `BLOCKED`: Dependent on another unresolved prerequisite.

---

## Decision Table

| ID | Topic | Decision / Proposal | Status | Reason | Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DEC-001** | Multi-Tenancy Isolation Strategy | Single MySQL database with strict application/data-layer scoping (`CompanyScope` + `TenantContext`). | **CONFIRMED** | SME Pakistani retail installment businesses have moderate data volume; multi-database adds severe migration and backup overhead. | Every tenant-owned table requires `company_id`. Zero trust on raw user-input tenant parameters. |
| **DEC-002** | Primary & Public Identifier Strategy | Dual-Key Strategy: `BIGINT UNSIGNED AUTO_INCREMENT` for internal joins + `ULID` (26 chars) for public URLs/APIs. | **CONFIRMED** | Prevents enumeration attacks and business volume leaks without suffering from MySQL UUIDv4 B-Tree index fragmentation. | Internal relations use integer foreign keys; external endpoints, QR codes, and URLs expose ULIDs. |
| **DEC-003** | Frontend Technology Stack | Laravel Blade + Livewire 3/4 + Bootstrap 5.3.8 (AppDashboard Pro). Zero React, Vue, Tailwind, or Inertia. | **CONFIRMED** | Preserves purchased AppDashboard template, eliminates duplicate frontend business logic, and ensures high development velocity. | Livewire components handle dynamic form calculations and real-time validations in PHP. |
| **DEC-004** | Multiple Agreements Per Customer | A customer can hold multiple concurrent or sequential installment agreements (e.g. Agreement #001 Completed, #015 Active). | **CONFIRMED** | Repeat customers are the foundation of retail installment growth; customers purchase additional electronics over time. | Customer-to-Agreement is a strict `1-to-many` relationship with a centralized Customer Credit Profile. |
| **DEC-005** | Customer Credit Profile & Repayment Score | A customer's repayment history directly drives future credit eligibility rather than a simple active/inactive boolean. | **CONFIRMED** | Essential for risk mitigation in high-value electronic financing. | Dynamic credit assessment score calculated from completed installments, days past due, and guarantor standing. |
| **DEC-006** | Financial Transaction Immutability | Prohibit hard updates or destructive deletes on financial records. Corrections must use Reversal / Adjustment Transactions. | **CONFIRMED** | Regulatory and financial audit compliance. Eliminates silent ledger tampering. | Ledger, payments, down payments, and receipts are append-only. Corrections generate reversing ledger lines. |
| **DEC-007** | Payment Method vs Payment Status Separation | Separate how money was paid (`cash`, `bank_transfer`, `cheque`) from verification status (`pending_verification`, `acknowledged`, `rejected`). | **CONFIRMED** | Field Collection Officers collect cash in person; branch accountants must formally acknowledge before funds post to drawer. | Receipts indicate submission status; installment ledger is updated only upon authorized acknowledgement. |
| **DEC-008** | Multi-Tier Pricing Calculation Audit Trail | Store Original Calculation, Negotiated Calculation, and Final Agreement with author, timestamp, and deviation rationale. | **CONFIRMED** | Prevents unauthorized salesperson discounts and allows company audits on margin slippage. | Agreement schema must store original markup, negotiated markup, approved discount, and authorizing user ID. |
| **DEC-009** | Serialized and Non-Serialized Inventory | Full dual-mode tracking: mandatory IMEI/Serial numbers for phones/laptops/appliances; batch tracking for accessories. | **CONFIRMED** | High-value electronics require warranty, theft reporting, and repossession tracking by distinct physical IMEI/Serial. | Inventory module must support distinct `SerializedItem` entities linked to products and agreements. |
| **DEC-010** | Multiple Guarantors Per Agreement | Whether an installment agreement can mandate/allow multiple guarantors (e.g. minimum 2 guarantors). | **REQUIRES BUSINESS DECISION** | Varies by retailer risk tolerance; some require 1 government employee guarantor, others require 2 personal references. | Schema will support `hasMany(Guarantor::class)`; business rule determines validation minimum (1 vs 2+). |
| **DEC-011** | Default & Debt Recovery Policy Stages | Exact time thresholds for recovery escalations (e.g., 7 days reminder, 30 days flagged, 60 days recovery visit, 90 days legal). | **REQUIRES BUSINESS DECISION** | Retailers operate different grace periods and recovery timelines based on product category (smartphones vs appliances). | Recovery engine rules must be company-configurable rather than hard-coded into code. |
| **DEC-012** | Accounting Depth: Payment Tracking vs Full GL | Whether Phase 07 implements simple installment ledger tracking or full double-entry General Ledger with Chart of Accounts. | **REQUIRES BUSINESS DECISION** | Deep double-entry accounting requires specialized SME chart-of-accounts setup; payment tracking satisfies 90% of daily ops. | Architectural constraint: data model must isolate payment ledger so double-entry postings can be bridged seamlessly later. |
| **DEC-013** | Field Collection Officer Mobile Capabilities | Advanced mobile features: real-time GPS tracking, visit check-in geo-fencing, customer photo capture, and digital signature. | **FUTURE** | High initial development complexity; core manual receipt and payment submission workflow must be stabilized first. | Kept as optional extension in Phase 05/Phase 08 mobile collector UI. |
| **DEC-014** | Late-Fee Calculation Rules & Automatic Application | Daily percentage penalty vs fixed monthly surcharge, and whether late fees apply automatically or require manual invocation. | **PROPOSED** | Automatic scheduler application ensures consistency, while configurable grace periods protect customer goodwill. | Scheduled daily console job `installments:apply-late-fees` applying company-specific parameters. |
| **DEC-015** | Late-Fee Waiver Audit Workflow | Any reduction or complete waiver of late fees must require managerial override with reason and immutable log. | **CONFIRMED** | Prevents cashiers/collectors from pocketing late fees or granting undocumented favors. | Dedicated `LateFeeWaiver` entity capturing agreement ID, original fee, waived amount, approving user, and reason. |
| **DEC-016** | Legal Document Template Generation | Dynamic PDF generation for Customer Application, Credit Approval, Contract Agreement, Delivery Note, and NOC Clearance. | **PROPOSED** | Retailers require printed Urdu/English legal stamp paper agreements and customer clearance certificates. | Dedicated `DocumentService` utilizing standard HTML-to-PDF engine with company-specific header templates. |
| **DEC-017** | SaaS Multi-Tenant Subscription Limits | Configurable limits per plan tier: User accounts, physical branches, active agreements, monthly transactions, and SMS quota. | **CONFIRMED** | Core monetization model for multi-tenant platform operator. | Platform administration module controls plan quotas via tenant middleware guards. |
